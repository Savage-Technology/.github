# Savage Tech 👋

**Building what's next in wearable computing.**

We're a team of engineers, designers, and builders rethinking how humans interact with technology.

At Savage Tech, we believe computing should feel natural, effortless, and always accessible.

Our first product, **Orrorin**, is a new kind of smart glasses designed for all-day use.

### What we're building

- 🕶️ **Orrorin Glasses** — Lightweight, full-color smart glasses built for everyday life.
- ⚡ **Efficient by design** — Ultra-low-power embedded computing, optimized from silicon to software.
- 🐧 **Linux at the core** — An open, developer-friendly platform with native C/C++ SDK.
- 🤏 **Natural interaction** — Gesture-first controls that make computing feel effortless.

### Our philosophy

**Less friction. More freedom.**

We believe the future of computing isn't another screen demanding your attention. It's technology that becomes a seamless part of your life.

We're building that future, one prototype at a time.
